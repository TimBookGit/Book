About the book

We All Carry Symbols

Some remind us where we’ve been. Others help us find our way forward.

The Symbols We Carry is a book for anyone who has experienced trauma. An inspirational toolkit with simple symbols to guide the reader on the path they walk to healing.

Tim Martin uses simple images — a mirror, a compass, a river, fishing line, and more — to break down lessons about self-reflection, protection, direction, hope, patience, and support for anyone wanting to learn more about surviving traumatic experiences.

This book is for anyone who has carried pain and uncertainty for themselves or loved ones, and a reminder to all: you are stronger than you realize.

About the author

Timothy Martin

Timothy Martin is a proud father, leader, advocate, and the founder of Symbolisms for Resilience, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to helping veterans, first responders, survivors, and anyone facing life's hardships find strength through connection, symbolism, and community.

The son of a United States Marine, Tim grew up all over the world as a military brat. For more than two decades he has built a career as an executive in government services, but the mission closest to his heart began with a single object: the Coin of Resilience, a tangible reminder to keep fighting, reach out, and never lose hope.
