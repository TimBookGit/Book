Read in full

This book resonates deeply because it affirms a truth that has shaped my life and work: symbols matter. Whether a challenge coin, a memorial, or a personal keepsake, they connect us to sacrifice, purpose, and hope. Tim turns these into compelling lessons, reminding us that resilience is not about avoiding the fall, but finding the strength to rise.

My grandfather, Medal of Honor recipient Hershel “Woody” Williams, often said, “The cause is greater than I.” This book reflects that spirit. It is a clear, encouraging reminder that pain can become purpose—and a light for others. I strongly endorse it for veterans, first responders, survivors, and anyone seeking healing and renewed purpose.”

“The Symbols We Carry captures something I have seen repeatedly in resilience and prevention work: healing rarely happens through information alone. It happens through meaning, connection, and the reminder that we do not have to carry difficult things by ourselves. Through deeply personal stories and simple, powerful symbols, Tim Martin gives readers tangible ways to recognize their own strength and, just as importantly, to help carry hope for someone else.”

Yael Schwarz Freimann

Resilience, Prevention & Community Impact Strategist
